How these numbers work

India time (Asia/Kolkata). Opens are successful video-details visits. Starts are app-reported playback starts. A qualified view is 30 watched seconds, or 90% of a shorter video. Each viewer contributes at most one qualified view per video per day. Unique viewers are daily estimates. Across a date range, the total adds distinct viewer-days. Guests who clear app data may count again. Creator self-views are excluded. Watch time is app-reported, bounded by elapsed server time, and excludes ads, buffering, pauses and background time. Seek distance adds no watch time. Counts begin with the analytics-enabled Android release and may be delayed or missing when offline. Sessions are assigned to their opening day. Up to 20,000 sessions per video per day are accepted. These figures are product analytics, not billing or AdMob earnings.

90-day server retention. Select up to 31 days at a time. Deleted videos retain their historical totals during retention.
